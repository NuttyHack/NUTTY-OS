NUTTY
The Personal AI Operating System
Product vision
Build NUTTY, a premium modern personal AI operating system designed to help a person understand, organize, plan, improve, and manage their life.
NUTTY is NOT simply:
•	a chatbot
•	a calendar
•	a fitness tracker
•	a productivity app
•	a school planner
•	a finance app
•	a to-do list
•	a voice assistant
NUTTY combines these capabilities into one intelligent personal system.
The long-term goal is:
NUTTY should understand my life well enough to help me decide what to do, when to do it, why it matters, and how to execute it.
The product should feel like a futuristic personal operating system rather than a traditional SaaS dashboard.
Think:
AI brain + personal memory + life database + planning engine + decision engine + execution layer + beautiful interface.
The user should eventually be able to say:
“NUTTY, what should I do right now?”
And NUTTY should consider the user's actual:
•	time
•	calendar
•	work
•	school
•	deadlines
•	tasks
•	goals
•	fitness
•	health-related information the user chooses to provide
•	sleep
•	energy
•	finances
•	location when explicitly authorized
•	travel
•	habits
•	preferences
•	commitments
•	available resources
•	priorities
•	previous behavior
•	current context
Then provide a useful next action.

1. ABSOLUTE PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
These rules are more important than individual features.
Rule 1 — NEVER INVENT THE USER
A new account must contain:
•	no fake name
•	no fake age
•	no fake weight
•	no fake goals
•	no fake schedule
•	no fake classes
•	no fake work
•	no fake workouts
•	no fake nutrition
•	no fake finances
•	no fake sleep
•	no fake readiness score
•	no fake progress
•	no fake achievements
If information does not exist, NUTTY must say that it does not know yet.
Example:
Bad:
“Your readiness is 86/100.”
Good:
“I don't have enough information to calculate your readiness yet. Tell me how you slept, your energy level, and whether you're sore.”
NUTTY earns knowledge.
